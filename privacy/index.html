Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Vine Studio is a web design and development studio based in Bhaderwah, Jammu and Kashmir, India. If you have a question about this policy or your information, email us at vinestudio.in@gmail.com.

What we receive

When you contact us on Instagram or by email, we may receive your name, messages, photos, logos and business details. We only receive the material you choose to send us.

What this website collects

If Google Analytics 4 is enabled on this website, it collects usage data such as pages visited, device information and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.

Why we use information

We use messages and project material to reply to you, build demos and complete projects. We use website usage data to understand how people use our site.

Services that handle information

Google handles Analytics data. Cloudflare hosts this website. Netlify may host a temporary demo. Each service handles information under its own privacy terms.

Free demo data

We use the photos, logos and details you send only to create your one-page demo. Demos are hosted temporarily for review and hidden from search engines. If you do not go ahead with a project, we delete the demo and the files you sent. If you do go ahead, we build the final website from scratch and delete the demo.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and project material only for as long as we need them to respond, build a demo, complete a project or meet our records needs. You can ask us to delete or correct your information at any time.

Your choices

We do not sell personal data. You can ask us to delete or correct information by emailing vinestudio.in@gmail.com. You can block analytics cookies through your browser settings or Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us about the personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it.

Children

Our website is not aimed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email vinestudio.in@gmail.com.
